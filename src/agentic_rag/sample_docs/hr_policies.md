# HR Policies

## Paid time off

Full-time employees accrue 1.5 days of paid time off per month, for a total of 18 days per year. Up to 5 unused days can be carried over into the next calendar year. Requests longer than 5 consecutive days need manager approval two weeks in advance.

## Remote work

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week. Fully remote arrangements require approval from the department head and HR.

## Learning budget

Each employee receives an annual learning budget of 1,200 USD for courses, certifications, and conferences. Unused budget does not roll over.
