# IT Support Handbook

## Password resets

Employees can reset their password through the self-service portal at any time. Passwords must be at least 14 characters and are rotated every 90 days. After 5 failed login attempts the account is locked for 15 minutes.

## VPN access

The corporate VPN is required when accessing internal systems from outside the office. Install the VPN client from the software center and sign in with single sign-on. VPN sessions disconnect automatically after 12 hours.

## Laptop replacement

Laptops are refreshed every 36 months. To request an early replacement, open a hardware ticket with the device serial number and a description of the issue. Replacement devices ship within 5 business days.
